Hand Weaving Experience in Gion, Kyoto

At BLANKED KYOTO, you can use a full-featured handloom suitable for both children and adults to make a bookmark, omamori charm or sacoche bag.

No previous experience is required. Our staff guide you through each step, and you can take your finished piece home on the same day.

A Quiet Moment of Weaving

Japan developed alongside its textile industries, but many people rarely have an opportunity to learn how everyday towels and clothing are made from individual threads.

Choose your yarns, sit at the loom and repeat the weaving movement row by row. Through plain weaving, the most fundamental weave structure, you can experience how fabric is created.

The quiet concentration of weaving can feel similar to the stillness associated with Zen.
