Hand Weaving Workshop in Gion, Kyoto

  1. Home
  2. Hand Weaving Experience

At BLANKED KYOTO you use a real table loom, suitable for both children and adults, to weave a bookmark, omamori charm or sacoche bag. It is an indoor, hands-on craft class you can fit into a day of Kyoto sightseeing — from just 30 minutes.

No previous experience is required. Our staff guide you through each step, and you take your finished piece home the same day. Ideal for solo travellers, families, couples and friends, and a good rainy-day plan in Gion.

A Quiet Moment of Weaving

Japan developed alongside its textile industries, but many people rarely have an opportunity to learn how everyday towels and clothing are made from individual threads.

Choose your yarns, sit at the loom, pass the shuttle and pull the beater, row by row. Through plain weave, the most fundamental weave structure, you experience how fabric is created.

The quiet concentration of weaving can feel similar to the stillness associated with Zen.

Quick FAQ

Can I join on a rainy day?

Yes. The workshop is entirely indoors, so weather is not a problem. We are 30 seconds from Exit 6 of Gion-Shijo Station, so you can reach us almost without getting wet.

Can I join without a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome when space is available. We have four looms and take up to four guests per session, so we recommend booking in advance.

Is the workshop available in English?

Yes. Our staff guide you in English. For other languages such as Chinese or Korean we use translation tools.
