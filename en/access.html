Finding the Entrance

We are on the second floor of the Gion Matsumoto Building. Look for our sign at the ground-floor entrance and take the stairs.

There is no elevator in the building. If stairs are difficult for you, please contact us in advance. We have no parking of our own; paid car parks are available nearby.
