Privacy Policy (Measurement & Cookies)

  2. Privacy Policy

Nakamori Co., Ltd. (“we”) operates the BLANKED KYOTO website at blankedkyoto.com (the “Site”). This policy explains how the Site uses cookies and measurement technologies.

1. Information we collect

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies for the purposes below. We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers or other directly identifying personal data on the Site.

AnalyticsGoogle Analytics / Google Tag Manager (Google LLC): page views and clicks on links to booking platforms.
Advertising measurementOpenAI Ads Measurement Pixel (OpenAI): whether visitors arriving from ads shown in ChatGPT reach our booking page. A click identifier (oppref) is stored in a cookie for up to 30 days and a browser identifier for up to 365 days.

2. Consent

Advertising measurement (OpenAI Ads Measurement Pixel) is enabled only after you choose “Accept” in the notice shown at the bottom of the Site. If you choose “Decline”, no measurement data is sent. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time (clearing the Site's cookies and site data will show the notice again).

3. Sharing with third parties

Collected data is transmitted to the providers above (Google LLC, OpenAI) and handled under their respective privacy policies. We do not share the data with any other third party except where required by law.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

4. External booking platforms

Bookings are made on Viator, Klook or Jalan.net (Recruit Co., Ltd.). Information you enter on those services is governed by their own privacy policies. Our Site's measurement does not capture anything you enter there.

5. Disabling cookies

You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so does not affect your ability to browse the Site or make a booking.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when laws or our measurement tools change. Updates take effect when posted on this page.
