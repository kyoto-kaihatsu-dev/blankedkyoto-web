BLANKED KYOTO — Hand Weaving Workshop & Gauze Shop in Gion

It begins with weaving.

BLANKED KYOTO is the directly operated store of Nakamori, a Japanese textile manufacturer whose roots date back to 1951. Thirty seconds from Exit 6 of Gion-Shijo Station in Kyoto's Gion district, we offer a hand weaving experience — choose from around 30 yarn colours and weave a bookmark, omamori charm or sacoche bag — and sell gauze textiles made at Nakamori's own factory.

  • From 30 minutes
  • Indoor — great on rainy days
  • Ages 6 and over
  • Nothing to bring
  • Solo guests welcome
  • Take your piece home the same day
  • English spoken
  • Small groups: max 4 per session

Hand Weaving Experience — Three Courses

Using a real handloom suitable for children and adults, you learn plain weave, the most fundamental weave structure. No experience is needed. Our staff guide you through every step, and you take your finished piece home the same day. Choose a 30-minute course to fit between sightseeing stops, or spend two hours on a bag.

Prices include tax. We have four looms and can host up to four guests per session across all courses.

Made by Nakamori

Nakamori produces gauze textiles at its own factory in Gamagori, Aichi. Its work covers product planning, weave design, yarn selection and sourcing, weaving, cutting, sewing, inspection, packaging and sales.

The company continues the multi-layer gauze weaving technique developed by its founder, Tsukasa Nakase. In our Gion shop you can handle handkerchiefs, face towels and gauze blankets before you buy — no reservation needed for shopping.
