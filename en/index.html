BLANKED KYOTO is the shop of Nakamori Co., Ltd., a Japanese textile maker specialising in gauze fabric. We are 30 seconds from Gion-Shijo Station. Choose your colours, work the loom, and leave with something you made yourself.

Inside you will find hand weaving experiences and our gauze textile products.
