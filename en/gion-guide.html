Gion Experience Guide

  1. Home
  2. Gion Experience Guide

A guide to experiences in and around Gion, organised by category: watching traditional performing arts, maiko dress-up, tea ceremony and wagashi sweets, a museum, and hands-on craft. Choose by what you would like to do - watch, taste, learn, or make something with your own hands to take home.

Watch: Traditional Performing Arts

Gion Corner

A stage programme at the Gion Kobu Kaburenjo theatre, where you can watch Kyoto-style dance, tea ceremony, koto music and other traditional Japanese performing arts. Recommended if you want to sample several traditional arts in a short time.

Miyako Odori

A spring stage performance by maiko and geiko, held every April at the Gion Kobu Kaburenjo theatre. Recommended for a classic Kyoto spring memory.

Maiko Experience

Maiko Experience Gion Aya

Try maiko-style makeup and kimono dressing and have commemorative photos taken. Recommended if you want to keep a travel memory of yourself in a completely different style.

Tea Ceremony & Wagashi

Tea Ceremony Camellia FLOWER

A tea ceremony experience in the Ninenzaka / Kiyomizu area, a short walk from Gion. Recommended for a calm moment with matcha and the atmosphere of the tea ceremony.

Kanshundo Higashi (Wagashi Making)

A wagashi (Japanese sweets) making experience in the Higashiyama area, a little further from Gion. Recommended if you want to make seasonal Kyoto sweets with your own hands.

Museum

Kanji Museum

A museum and library where you can see, touch and learn how kanji characters were formed and the culture behind them. It is indoors, so it is also a good option for families and rainy days.

Hands-on Craft

BLANKED KYOTO - Hand Weaving Workshop

Our own hand weaving workshop. Choose from around 30 yarn colours and weave on a real table loom: a bookmark (about 30 minutes), a marker charm (about 45 minutes), an omamori charm (about 60 minutes) or a sacoche bag (about 120 minutes). You finish your piece on the spot and take it home the same day. The workshop is indoors and open to ages 6 and up, 30 seconds from Exit 6 of Gion-Shijo Station.

Combining Experiences

A short hands-on session fits easily around performances and sightseeing. For example, the 30-minute bookmark course after a visit to Yasaka Shrine (about 5 minutes from our shop), or the 60-minute omamori course before an evening performance.

Please check each facility's official information for performance dates, experience details, prices, opening hours and how to book before you visit.
