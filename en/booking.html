Cancellation

Cancellation is free until 20:00 on the day before your session (this applies on every booking platform). After that, the cancellation terms of the platform you booked through apply.

Capacity

We have four looms. The limit of four guests per session applies to everyone in that session regardless of course, and different courses can run side by side. Groups of five or more are split across consecutive sessions.

A sacoche booking occupies a loom through the following session.
